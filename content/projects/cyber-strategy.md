**[Play Cyber Strategy →](https://mkmodern.replit.app/)**

Before the web there were bulletin boards, and on the bulletin boards there were door games. Metal Knights was one of them: you dialled in, took your turn, hung up, and spent the rest of the day wondering what the other players had done to you. This is my modern take on it.

Command your units, conquer the grid. You can battle the computer in a single-player match with no login, or go online and get auto-matched against a real person. There's a how-to-play, a leaderboard, and a shop.

It is not bug free. If people play it, I'll fix it — so if something breaks, [tell me](/contact).
