The Ottawa high-tech meltdown of 2001 put half the city's tech workers on the street. My reaction was to start a company. Flick Software ran out of the top floor of my apartment, staffed by recently unemployed friends, with a first contract building a security tracking system. It grew into a mobile solutions shop with clients like Intel and Rogers Wireless — and it's where Stuart Russell and I met and spun up You.i Labs. In 2017 it spun off Sheldon, a resource-management tool.
