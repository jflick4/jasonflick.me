Large language models learned from text. The interesting question is what a model learns when it has to *act* — see something, do something, see what changed, and try again. So I built the smallest loop I could: a Dobot Magician arm, a camera pointed at a screen, and a vision model deciding what to do next.

The current objective — playing a mobile game on an iPad — is deliberately silly. It's a cheap, endless source of "did that work?" signals. The goal is the model, not the game.
