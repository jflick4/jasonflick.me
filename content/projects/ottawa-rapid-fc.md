I'm part of the founding ownership group of Ottawa Rapid FC, the capital's team in the Northern Super League — Canada's first professional women's soccer league. The club plays at TD Place and finished second in the league's inaugural 2025 season.

It's so cool to see women in Canada finally being paid to play. And they're so much fun to watch.
