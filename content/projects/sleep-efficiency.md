Andrew Holmes ran the sleep lab at the Queensway Carleton Hospital and started Sleep Efficiency as a side hustle because the wait for a sleep test was absurd. The company sends you a device, you sleep in your own bed, and a clinician reads the results. I came in as an investor and adviser and now serve as Executive Chairman.
