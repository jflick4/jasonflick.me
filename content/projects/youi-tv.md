At the dawn of the iPhone era the idea was simple and slightly mad: bring video-game thinking to TV apps, and own the glass. You.i Engine let media companies ship one beautiful app to every screen — phones, Roku, consoles, set-top boxes — from a single codebase.

By the end the company was 200-plus people in Kanata, shipping tier-one apps for WarnerMedia, AT&T, Fox, Corus, Rogers and others. WarnerMedia went from customer to investor (2016) to owner (December 2020). I left in January 2021. It's my third child, and I don't regret a thing.
